Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains how AK Gaming ("we") handles information in our app Click Blocks (package: com.jagmohansinghsardar.clickblocks).

Information we collect

We do not ask for your email, contacts, photos or precise location. Your settings, coins and game progress are saved on your device.

World leaderboard

If you create a player profile, the app sends the following to our online leaderboard (Google Firebase): the player name you choose, the avatar you pick, your country (worked out from your device's time zone, and you can change it), and your best score and level. These are linked to a random anonymous ID that Firebase creates for your device. We do not link this ID to your Google account or any other personal identity.

Your player name, avatar, country, score and level are shown publicly to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use your real full name if you do not want it to be public. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us with your player name.

Advertising

The app shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device information, such as the advertising ID, IP address and app usage data, to show and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services

Google Play Services, Google AdMob and Google Firebase have their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page.
